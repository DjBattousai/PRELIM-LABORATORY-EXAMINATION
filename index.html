About Me

My name is Jerald L. Tucio, a 22‑year‑old BSIT student currently enrolled at Pamantasan ng Cabuyao. I live in Cabuyao City, Laguna, and I am passionate about technology and continuous learning. I believe that perseverance and dedication are the keys to achieving success in the IT industry.

Today, I am pursuing my degree at Pamantasan ng Cabuyao, where I continue to sharpen my skills and knowledge in information technology.

My career goal is to become a successful IT professional who contributes to innovation and problem‑solving in the tech industry. I aspire to use my skills not only to build efficient systems but also to help others through technology. Guided by the motto, “It does not matter how slowly you go as long as you do not stop,” I remain committed to growth and excellence.
